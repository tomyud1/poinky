Problem Set 3

Derivatives and projectile motion (a sample sheet for tests)

Question 1

Let f(x) = x³ − 6x² + 9x + 2.

  1. (a) Find f′(x).
  2. (b) Find the critical points of f and classify each one as a local maximum or a local minimum.
  3. (c) Find the largest and smallest values of f on the interval [0, 4], and where they occur.

Question 2

A ball is thrown from the edge of a cliff 20 m high, with a speed of 15 m/s at 30° above the horizontal. Take g = 9.8 m/s² and ignore air resistance.

  1. (a) How long does the ball take to reach its highest point?
  2. (b) What is the greatest height of the ball above the ground?
  3. (c) How far from the foot of the cliff does the ball land?
