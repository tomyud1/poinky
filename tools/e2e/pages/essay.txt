My summer job

This summer I worked at a small bakery in my town, and it was honestly a really great experience that taught me a lot of things about work and also about myself and about people in general. At first I was really nervous because I had never had a job before and I did not know what to expect at all, but the owner, who is called Mrs. Alvarez, was very kind and patient with me and she showed me how to do everything step by step, from opening the shop early in the morning to closing up at night and cleaning all of the machines. By the end of the summer I could run the register on my own and I even helped decorate cakes for two weddings, which was something I never thought I would be able to do.

The hardest part was waking up at five in the morning. I learned that being on time matters more than being perfect.
