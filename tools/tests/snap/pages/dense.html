Cell biology: reading notes

Cells are the basic unit of life, and every living thing is made of one or more of them. The cell membrane controls what enters and leaves the cell, letting nutrients in and waste out while keeping harmful substances away from the interior. Inside, the nucleus stores the genetic material and directs the activity of the whole cell.

Organelles each have a job:

  • Ribosomes build proteins from amino acids
  • Mitochondria release energy from glucose
  • Chloroplasts capture light in plant cells
  • Vacuoles store water and dissolved salts
