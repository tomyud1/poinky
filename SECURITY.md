# Security

Poinky sees your screen, hears your questions, and can click, type and start coding agents when you ask it to. Please report anything that could let it act without your consent, leak what's on your screen, or get around its safety checks.

**Report privately:** use GitHub's "Report a vulnerability" button (Security tab). Please don't open a public issue for security problems.

What counts as in scope:
- Clicking, typing or menu actions that bypass the spoken confirmation, the password-field check, or the blocked shortcuts
- Screen content or instructions on a web page that make Poinky act on its own (prompt injection)
- Background agents running commands outside their allowed list
- Captures of apps on the privacy exclusion list
