# Code of Conduct

Poinky is a small, friendly project. Everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests and discussions) is expected to:

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to the project or to coding.
- Disagree about ideas, not about people. No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly what you mean.
- Respect other people's privacy. Don't share someone's personal information, or screenshots that show it.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules.

**Report a problem** with someone's behavior through GitHub's "Report content" option, or privately to the maintainer ([@tomyud1](https://github.com/tomyud1)) using the repository's "Report a vulnerability" button (Security tab), which is private.

This is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
