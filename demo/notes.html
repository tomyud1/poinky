Lake Ardmore camping trip

📅 Dates
Oct 17 → Oct 19
👥 Going
SamJuniperYou
📍 Site
North shore, pitch 14
⏳ Status
Planning

Two nights at the north shore. We drive up Friday after work, hike to the upper lake on Saturday (about 11 km round trip) and pack up Sunday morning.

Before we go

1.✓Check the weather forecast for Saturday and Sunday night.
2.✓Charge the headlamps and the power bank, and pack spare batteries.
3.Buy food for three dinners, plus snacks for the hike up to the lake.
4.Confirm the campsite booking and print the parking permit for the dashboard.
5.Pack the first-aid kit: bandages, blister plasters, painkillers, tweezers.
6.Tell someone at home the route and when you expect to be back.
+ Add a to-do
⚠️
Fire ban until Oct 31. Gas stoves are fine, but no campfires. The ranger said fines start at $250.
