Weird result from our 3D-printed backyard wind turbine 🌬️ At 8 m/s wind the 3-blade rotor made 42 W. We printed a 5-blade rotor expecting MORE power… and got 35 W. More blades, less power?! 🧵 what we learned below
2/ Each extra blade adds drag. At 8 m/s our 3-blade rotor spun at the right speed for its size. The 5-blade one spun slower and the extra blades kept getting in each other's wake.
3/ Rule of thumb we found: more blades = more torque at low speed (great for water pumps), fewer blades = more power at higher wind. Next we're trying a 2-blade rotor. 🛠️
